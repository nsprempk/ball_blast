Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ball Blast. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Ball Blast mobile game.

Ball Blast is provided as a free mobile game and may display advertisements to support the game.

2. Information and Data

Ball Blast does not require you to create an account to play the game.

The game itself may store gameplay-related information locally on your device, such as game progress, settings or in-game rewards.

We do not ask you to provide your name, phone number, home address or password in order to play Ball Blast.

3. Advertising

Ball Blast is supported by advertising. Advertisements may appear during gameplay and may include banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements and rewarded advertisements.

Advertising services may collect information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location and information about ad interactions in accordance with their own policies and applicable laws.

We use Google AdMob to provide advertisements in the game.

4. Rewarded Advertisements

Ball Blast may provide optional rewarded advertisements. If you choose to watch a rewarded advertisement, you may receive an in-game reward after the advertisement has completed according to the game's reward rules.

Watching rewarded advertisements is optional.

5. Third-Party Services

Ball Blast may use third-party services to provide advertising and related functionality.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Google AdMob is one of the services used by Ball Blast for advertising.

6. Children's Privacy

Ball Blast is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the game. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
